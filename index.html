    fig. 01 · Now

    Building AI tools people actually use.

    I am a software engineer at ByteDance, building developer tools for an AI model platform and creative tools where people and AI make content together.

    Before that, I spent five years at Eindhoven University of Technology (PhD and postdoc) building algorithms and interactive tools that help people understand, compare, and debug AI models — and studying how they actually use them, from ML practitioners to clinicians.

    → next

    Where I want to go next: human–AI collaboration.

    Building tools where people and AI create together, and earlier studying how clinicians use AI tools in practice, left me with one question I keep coming back to: how can people and AI agents create, reason, and make decisions together? That is the research direction I want to pursue next.

    fig. 02 · Work

    Data, interaction, and the people in between.

    I work where AI meets data and people: tools that let people inspect and evaluate the data and models behind AI, and interfaces that let them question, steer, and work with it. Interactive visualization and user studies are my main methods.

    01

    Data & evaluation tools

    Tools for finding what is wrong in the data and models behind AI: hard instances, abnormal clients in federated learning, long-running training experiments, and side-by-side model comparison.

    PhD & postdoc, TU/e · Oxford e-Research Centre · Zhejiang University
    02

    Interaction & human–AI collaboration

    Interactive systems and user studies: how clinicians and ML practitioners use AI tools, what-if questions with counterfactuals, LLM-written explanations of data, and creative tools where people and AI make content together.

    EU SmartCHANGE · ELLIIT, Linköping · ByteDance
    03

    Platforms & developer tooling

    Developer tools for an AI model platform, and earlier, dashboards, a visualization component library, and open-source visualization for SynBioHub.

    ByteDance · Cisco · Zhejiang University · Google Summer of Code

    ModelWise

    Compare models, find why they fail

    An interactive multi-model comparison tool that helps ML practitioners diagnose performance gaps, spot failure patterns, and choose models. Validated through user studies with practitioners.

    First authorPaperCodeDemoVideo
    2024 · IEEE TVCG / VIS 2023

    Class-Constrained t‑SNE

    Class-aware maps of ML datasets

    A dimensionality reduction algorithm that combines data features with class probabilities, so a single 2D map shows both what the data looks like and what the model thinks — scaling to millions of points.

    First authorPaperCode
    2021 · ACM TiiS

    VADAF

    Spotting abnormal clients in federated learning

    A visual analytics tool for detecting and explaining abnormal clients in federated learning — without access to their private data.

    First authorPaper
    2021 · ACS Synthetic Biology (VisBOL2)

    SynBioHub Sequence View

    Google Summer of Code, NRNB

    A JavaScript sequence visualization layer for DNA designs, following the SBOL Visual standard and integrated into the SynBioHub production platform.

    Open-source contributorCodeWikiPaper
    2018 · IEEE TVCG / VAST 2018

    Visual Abstraction of OD Movement

    Millions of trips, one readable map

    A geospatial visualization system that abstracts and renders millions of origin–destination movement records. Second author and core team member.
